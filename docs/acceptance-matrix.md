## Priority findings from independent source review

1. **P1 - a selected-range summary can report the full-run loss.** Baseline `RunDetailPage` falls back to `run.loss` while range data is pending or failed, but caption already says “In selected range”. An invalid-only range can initially look finite. Query keys alone do not prevent this mixed-scope presentation. Requested-range summaries must be unavailable until current-scope data exists, or display explicitly previous bounds while updating.
2. **P1 - keyboard skip navigation changes the route.** `href="#main"` conflicts with the application's HashRouter. Preserve the route while focusing/scrolling the main region.
3. **P1 candidate - singleton finite segment can disappear.** Baseline uPlot points are enabled only when total series length is less than two. For `[null, 9.4, null]`, retaining the spike does not guarantee a visible path/marker. Confirm with actual Canvas pixels and render finite singleton segments without enabling every raw point marker.
4. **P2 - offscreen mobile controls remain focusable.** A translated sidebar is visually closed but still in tab order. It needs conditional hidden/inert semantics, open-state focus containment and focus restoration.
5. **P2 - source legibility is below ordinary review expectations.** The domain/data split is useful, but run-detail orchestration and JSX are compressed into very long lines. Format and extract meaningful responsibilities such as range state, summary scope, bookmarks/export and section rendering, without adding unnecessary layers.

## Baseline evidence boundaries

The 41 unit tests and 13 E2E scenarios are real, but their count is not a quality score. Worker-unit tests use a fake Worker; real-browser tests use Chromium only. The mobile scenario is viewport emulation, not a physical device. There was no sustained memory/leak run, broad random data exploration, or complete screen-reader assessment in the baseline. The application's first-render/zoom/hover sample is one observation including automation transport; it must not be relabeled as a statistically robust distribution or conflated with the isolated fixture.

The measured zoom regression (6.9ms raw baseline versus 33.3ms worker/sampled fixture) is an engineering trade-off to investigate and disclose, not an automatic disqualification. The assignment explicitly values clear negative results. Preserve source data truth, reproduce the measurements and explain why the chosen rendering approach is adequate for its measured use.

### Technical verdict and remaining delivery gates

No remaining P1 defect was found in this final read-only review of the requested generated-data monitor. The corrected implementation and observed integration checks form a credible technical submission candidate within the PDF's scope. Real training-cluster authentication or control is not a missing take-home requirement and must not be invented as a reason to reject the submitted frontend.

The **complete submission is still open** at this review point: the final updated performance repetitions and 180-second interaction/memory collection are in progress, the two-page note must be reconciled with their actual results, and a repository plus verified Vercel/GitHub Pages URL remain outstanding. Do not mark those gates passed from source configuration or a local preview. Retain negative measurements, failed attempts and the distinction between fixture-only and full-application timing. External publication and any public source visibility still require the user's authorization.

A 180-second soak, if successful, will provide bounded evidence for that interaction sequence and duration; it cannot prove that no leak appears after hours or days. Chromium, Firefox and Playwright WebKit are actual tested engines, while a real iPhone, installed Safari, browser extensions, screen readers and arbitrary device hardware remain outside the observed matrix. The source remains deterministic synthetic data and does not certify a real ingestion service's freshness or scientific validity. The PDF allocates 35% to the human live discussion: readable architecture and preserved evidence support it, but no test suite or AI report can establish the candidate's understanding or guarantee hiring.

## Reopened retention failure after the short-suite pass

The source review and 132-execution short suite did not establish sustained-use safety. The interim cache-pruning candidate failed the subsequent real-UI soak: main-page retained heap reached about 461.69 MB at 34 seconds while canonical worker backing stayed 32.32 MB and visible chart/listener counts stayed stable. A new real `QueryObserver` regression failed at transition 12 because the active query had been removed from the cache. The custom synchronous pruner was removed in favor of native `gcTime: 10_000`; the same regression then passed 40 transitions.

The fixed-build 30-second active / 15-second idle probe completed 24 iterations across all 20 runs with zero browser errors: combined page/worker V8 and backing stores 38.92 -> 43.41 MB peak -> 40.34 MB after idle. This closes the short reproduction, while a new full browser matrix, final-build performance evidence and 180-second soak are still required before final release. Original failures and allocation evidence remain under `evidence/round2/retention/` and `evidence/round2/soak/`.
